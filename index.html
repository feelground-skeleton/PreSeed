What FeelGround Is

A mental wellness ecosystem combining structured programs, trained human facilitators, and plant-based supplement support — in one integrated platform that adapts as users evolve. The system does the work. Users don't have to.

We sit in the whitespace between clinical treatment and abandonment. Individual therapy is expensive, often inaccessible, and not designed for ongoing support across life's phases. Group programs and platform memberships change that equation entirely — making consistent, structured mental wellness support financially sustainable for a far wider population without compromising on quality or depth. More people supported. Better outcomes at scale.

FeelGround also solves a supply-side gap — connecting gifted facilitators, coaches, and healers who have walked the journey themselves to a wider audience and sustainable income. People who speak directly to who their clients are, in ways traditional models never could. FeelGround gives them the platform, the structure, and the reach that would otherwise take years and significant resources to build independently.

Built for the Global South first — South Africa, Africa, India — markets that have been left behind by global wellness platforms. Long-term vision: the defining mental wellness operating system for the majority of the world that existing platforms have never truly reached.

Current Strategic Focus

1 — Products to shelves. Getting the FeelGround supplement range fully licensed so it can be stocked by doctors, clinicians, and facilitators who want to integrate it into their practice. An affiliate program is already designed and ready to activate the moment licensing is in place.

2 — Program refined and data collected. Completing and refining our flagship Depression Navigation Program — collecting rigorous outcome data before moving into the next stage of development. This evidence base is the foundation everything else is built on.

3 — Platform built with partners. Once product and program are proven, we move into the full platform build with interested partners — a more robust, proprietary system informed by real-world data and built for scale.
